The fonts are shipped for SPARTA's two Dyslexic themes and are loaded
privately at startup (FR_PRIVATE - nothing is installed on the system).
Swapping the typeface: drop any other family's .ttf/.otf files in this
folder and adjust DYS_FONT_FILES / _resolve_dyslexic_font() in app.py.
